Thread on why most "AI strategies" are garbage:

1/ Companies are spending millions on AI initiatives that amount to "let's add a chatbot." That's not strategy. That's a checkbox.

2/ Real AI strategy starts with one question: what decision does this help us make better?

Not faster. Not cheaper. BETTER.

3/ If you can't answer that question in one sentence, you don't have a strategy. You have a PowerPoint deck.

4/ The companies winning with AI right now aren't the ones with the biggest budgets. They're the ones who picked ONE painful process and rebuilt it from scratch.

5/ Stop trying to "implement AI across the organization." Pick your ugliest workflow. Fix that. Then expand.
